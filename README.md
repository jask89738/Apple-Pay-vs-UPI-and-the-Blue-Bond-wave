Why doesn't UPI work on Apple Pay?
Apple Pay is a card-based wallet. It stores a token of your Visa or Mastercard credit card and works with NFC tap machines. UPI is a separate bank-to-bank system run by NPCI, built around QR codes and UPI PINs.

The launch was made possible because the RBI allowed biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID) for card payments last year — removing the need for OTPs.

Apple told CNBC TV18: “UPI is a fantastic product. We would love to support it one day.” Talks with HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank were ongoing at launch.

New in India · Sustainable finance

India's first Blue Bond
Blue bond facts last verified 3 October 2026, 12:08 PM IST
A blue bond works like a normal bond — investors lend money and earn interest — but the money must go only to ocean and water projects: ports, fisheries, coastal protection, clean water and marine energy.

Issuer
Sagarmala Finance Corporation (Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways)
Size
₹600 crore base + ₹500 crore greenshoe (up to ₹1,100 crore)
Term & rating
10-year bonds, rated AA+ by ICRA and CARE; arranged by SBI Capital Markets
Launch date
Scheduled 28 September 2026
Uses
Greenfield ports, coastal roads, inland waterways, eco-friendly dredging, green shipbuilding
Next up
Vadodara Municipal Corporation plans a ~₹200 crore blue bond for water infrastructure
Why it matters
SEBI recognises blue bonds as sustainable finance. Sagarmala's projects take ~12 years to pay back, but its borrowings average 3.5 years — a long 10-year bond fixes that gap and attracts insurers and pension funds.

Watch out for
No universal "blue" rulebook exists yet, so strict reporting is needed to avoid "bluewashing". The issue is just 0.17% of Sagarmala's ₹6 lakh crore project pipeline — its value is the precedent.
